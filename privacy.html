VanoAtlas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

VanoAtlas is a personal research-tracking tool (tasks, notes, agenda, finance, and personal journaling) that runs entirely in your web browser. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

Who operates this app

VanoAtlas is an independent, non-commercial project. Contact: y.aitmohamed@yahoo.com.

What data VanoAtlas touches

When you sign in with your Google Account, VanoAtlas asks for two permissions:

  • Google Drive — app data only (drive.appdata): VanoAtlas stores your tasks, projects, notes, agenda entries, finance records, and journal entries in a single hidden file inside a special folder of your own Google Drive, reserved for this app. This folder is not visible in your normal Drive file list and cannot be browsed by other apps. VanoAtlas cannot see, list, or touch any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar — read only (calendar.readonly): if you use the "Sync Google Calendar" button, VanoAtlas reads your upcoming calendar events so they can be shown alongside your other research data in the Agenda view. VanoAtlas never creates, edits, or deletes anything in your Google Calendar.

Where your data lives

Nowhere but your own Google Drive. VanoAtlas has no backend server, no database, and no analytics service of its own. Every request made by the app goes directly from your browser to Google's servers (googleapis.com), using a temporary access token tied to your Google sign-in. Nobody who runs or maintains VanoAtlas — including its author — can read, access, or recover your data. If you lose access to your Google Account, your data is not recoverable by us, because we never had a copy of it.

Local storage on your device

VanoAtlas keeps a local cache of your data in your browser's localStorage so the app loads instantly and still shows your last-known data if you're briefly offline. This cache never leaves your device and is cleared when you sign out.

What VanoAtlas does not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no sale of data to third parties.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No sharing of your data with any other user of the app — each person's data lives only in their own Google Drive.
  • No server-side storage, backup, or copy of your data anywhere.

Revoking access

You can disconnect VanoAtlas at any time from inside the app ("Sign out"), or by removing its access entirely from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access does not delete the data file already stored in your Drive's app-data folder; you can delete it yourself the same way you'd delete any file you no longer want.

Children's privacy

VanoAtlas is a general-purpose productivity tool not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
